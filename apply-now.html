Before You Apply

What South African applicants should have ready

Having these on hand — or in progress — will help your application move faster:

01

A valid South African passport

Your passport should have enough validity remaining to cover your visa and travel — if it's close to expiring, start renewing now via the Department of Home Affairs.

02

A SAPS Police Clearance Certificate

Most H-2A visa applications require a police clearance certificate from the South African Police Service. This can take several weeks, so it's worth applying early.

03

Your work experience and skills

Basic personal and contact details, plus a summary of any farm, driving, mechanical, or general labor experience you have.

04

Availability for the upcoming season

Let us know your earliest available start date and how long you're able to commit to a seasonal contract.

Our team will walk you through the medical exam, U.S. Embassy or Consulate interview, and any additional H-2A documentation once your application is received — you don't need everything finalized before you apply.
