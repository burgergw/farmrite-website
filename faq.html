FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what South African applicants ask us most — from H-2A eligibility and embassy interviews to police clearance certificates and what working with Farmrite actually costs.

Can South Africans apply for H-2A visas?

Yes. The H-2A program has no nationality restriction that excludes South Africans — you're eligible to apply as long as you meet the standard requirements: a valid passport, the ability to pass the required medical exam and police clearance check, and a genuine seasonal agricultural job offer from a U.S. employer. Farmrite specializes in matching South African applicants with employers who are set up to sponsor H-2A workers from South Africa.

How does the Farmrite job placement process work?

You start by applying through our free online form. From there, we match your skills and experience with seasonal farm job opportunities in the USA, help you prepare your documentation and police clearance certificate, guide you through the H-2A visa application and embassy interview, and support you with travel arrangements and orientation before you depart South Africa.

Do I need to attend a U.S. Embassy interview in South Africa?

Most H-2A visa applicants are required to attend an in-person interview at the nearest U.S. Embassy or Consulate. Farmrite helps you book and prepare for this interview, including what documents to bring and what questions to expect, so you go in ready.

What is a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate and do I need one?

A Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) from the South African Police Service confirms your criminal record status and is a standard requirement for most U.S. visa applications, including H-2A. It can take several weeks to process, so we recommend applying for yours as early as possible — our team will let you know exactly when to start.

What types of seasonal farm jobs are available through Farmrite?

Farmrite places workers into a wide range of seasonal agricultural roles, including crop farming, fruit and vegetable harvesting, equipment operation, irrigation, greenhouse and nursery work, vineyard and orchard work, packing houses, and livestock care. See our full list of job types for more detail.

What qualifications do I need to apply for seasonal farm work through Farmrite?

Requirements vary by role and employer, but most positions call for physical stamina, a willingness to work outdoors in different weather conditions, and a strong work ethic. Some roles — like equipment operation or mechanical work — benefit from specific experience, which we'll factor in when matching you to openings.

How much can I earn on an H-2A seasonal farm job?

H-2A employers are legally required to pay at least the minimum wage rate set for the job and location, paid in US dollars, and to meet specific standards for housing, transportation, and working conditions. Exact pay varies by state, crop, and role — your recruiter will walk you through the pay and conditions for any position before you accept it.

What kind of support can I expect from Farmrite during my placement?

We support you from your first application through to the end of your contract: documentation and visa guidance, embassy interview preparation, travel arrangements, pre-departure orientation, and ongoing communication with you and your employer once you're working in the USA.

Is there a cost involved in using Farmrite's services?

No. Farmrite's services are free for workers — our fees are covered by the employers we partner with, so you can rely on our support without any financial burden.

Is Farmrite a legitimate recruitment agency?

Farmrite works within the official, government-regulated H-2A visa framework and only places workers into legally contracted seasonal farm jobs with vetted U.S. employers. We never ask applicants to pay a fee to apply or be placed — if anyone asks you for money to secure an H-2A job, treat it as a red flag.
