About Farmrite

A recruitment partner built around one job: getting South Africans legally placed in seasonal farm work in the USA.

Farmrite specializes in placing South African workers into seasonal H-2A farm jobs with reputable employers across the United States. We built our process around the specific steps a South African applicant needs to take — a valid passport, a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate, a medical exam, and a U.S. Embassy or Consulate visa interview — so nothing catches you off guard along the way.

The H-2A program is a legal, government-regulated route to seasonal work in the USA: your employer is required to cover certain costs, pay at least the legally set wage for the role, and provide the working conditions set out in your job contract. We help you understand exactly what you're signing up for before you accept a position.

Our team works with employers across crop farming, livestock, orchards, vineyards, and nurseries, matching your skills and experience — whether you've driven a tractor, worked a harvest, or are applying for your first seasonal farm job — with a suitable opening.

Support doesn't stop once your visa is approved. We help with travel arrangements out of South Africa, explain what to expect on arrival in the USA, and stay in contact with you and your employer throughout your contract to help resolve any issues that come up.

There is no cost to apply — our services are free for workers, with our fees covered by the employers we partner with. If you're a South African looking for a legal way to work seasonal farm jobs in the USA, we're here to help you get there.
