The Process

How it works for South African applicants

A clear path from South Africa to seasonal farm work in the USA — from your first application to your U.S. Embassy interview, travel, and ongoing support once you're working. Here's exactly what to expect at each step.

1

Initial Consultation and Application

  • You apply through our free online form, sharing your qualifications, availability, and job preferences.
  • A member of our team reviews your application and reaches out to talk through the process and answer any questions.
  • We guide you through the paperwork specific to South African applicants — passport details, work history, and initial H-2A documentation.
2

Job Matching and Pre-Screening

  • We match your skills, experience, and preferences against our network of U.S. employers and current H-2A job openings.
  • As part of pre-screening, we verify references and assess relevant skills to ensure you're presented as a strong candidate.
  • This preparation helps South African applicants stand out to employers who are actively hiring H-2A workers.
3

Documentation, Police Clearance, and Medical Exam

  • Once matched with a job, we help you gather the documents South African applicants specifically need: a valid passport, a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate, and a medical exam with an approved physician.
  • We explain each form and requirement in plain language and keep track of timelines so nothing expires or gets missed.
  • We stay in contact with you, the employer, and the relevant authorities throughout this stage.
4

Job Offer, Visa Application, and Embassy Interview

  • You'll receive a formal job offer setting out work location, start date, duration, and wages, which you can review before accepting.
  • We help you complete the H-2A visa application and prepare for your interview at the nearest U.S. Embassy or Consulate in South Africa.
  • We brief you on what to bring and what to expect, so the interview isn't the first time you're hearing about the process.
5

Travel Arrangements and Orientation

  • Once your visa is approved, we help arrange your flight out of South Africa and coordinate onward transport to your job site or accommodation.
  • Before you depart, we run through your rights, responsibilities, and expectations as an H-2A worker, plus what to pack and what daily life looks like.
  • This session also covers accommodation, pay schedules, and general orientation to living and working in the USA.
6

Departure and Arrival

  • You travel from South Africa to the USA following the itinerary we've arranged.
  • On arrival, our team or local partners help you get from the airport to your accommodation or directly to the job site.
  • We make sure any last-minute questions or logistics are handled before you start work.
7

Job Placement and Ongoing Support

  • Once you begin your seasonal farm job, we stay in regular contact with you and your employer to make sure the placement is going well.
  • Our team remains available to help with any work-related issues, questions, or concerns that come up during your contract.
  • We help you stay compliant with H-2A program rules so your placement — and any future seasons — stays on track.
