The Process

How it works

From your first application in South Africa to ongoing support once you're working in the USA, here's what the Farmrite process looks like.

01

Application and Documentation

Apply through our free online form. We'll guide you through the required documents — a valid South African passport, a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate, a medical exam, and any other H-2A paperwork.

02

Job Placement

Our team matches your skills, experience, and preferences with available seasonal farm job opportunities in the USA, working with a network of employers who actively hire South African H-2A workers.

03

Visa Assistance

We guide you through the H-2A visa application and your U.S. Embassy or Consulate interview, helping you prepare the right forms and supporting documents so nothing holds up your approval.

04

Pre-Departure Preparation

As your departure date approaches, we help arrange flights out of South Africa, explain what to expect on arrival, and run through accommodation, pay, and workplace basics before you fly.

05

Arrival and Onboarding

Once you land in the USA, our team or local partners are there to help you get to your accommodation or job site and settle in for your first days of work.

06

Ongoing Support

Throughout your contract, we stay in touch, liaise with your employer on your behalf, and help resolve any work-related issues that come up.

07

Program Compliance

We help you stay compliant with H-2A program rules — legal status, work hours, and conditions — so your placement stays on track and above board for both you and your employer.
