Open Roles

H-2A job types we recruit South Africans for

Farmrite places South African workers across a wide range of seasonal farm roles in the USA. Specific openings, locations, and start dates vary by employer and season — apply now and our team will match you with current opportunities.

No. 01Field Crew

General Farm Laborer

Planting, cultivating, weeding, and hand-harvesting a variety of row crops.

No. 02Field Crew

Fruit and Vegetable Harvester

Hand-harvesting fruits and vegetables to grade, ripeness, and timing standards.

No. 03Equipment

Tractor and Farm Equipment Operator

Operating tractors, tillers, and field equipment for tilling, planting, and cultivation.

No. 04Field Crew

Irrigation Technician

Installing, monitoring, and maintaining irrigation systems and equipment across fields.

No. 05Field Crew

Crop Scout / Field Inspector

Monitoring crop health, pests, and disease, and recording field data for analysis.

No. 06Nursery

Greenhouse and Nursery Worker

Planting, potting, and tending seedlings and nursery stock under cover.

No. 07Field Crew

Vineyard Worker

Pruning, training, and hand-harvesting grapevines across the growing season.

No. 08Field Crew

Orchard Worker

Pruning, thinning, and hand-picking tree fruit during harvest periods.

No. 09Packing

Packing House / Sorting Worker

Grading, sorting, and packing harvested produce for shipment.

No. 10Equipment

Agricultural Equipment Mechanic

Maintaining and repairing tractors, harvesters, and other field machinery.

No. 11Equipment

Sprayer / Applicator Operator

Applying fertilizers, herbicides, and pesticides to crops under label guidance.

No. 12Livestock

Livestock and Range Worker

Herding and caring for livestock on open range under seasonal work contracts.

No. 13Supervision

Crew Leader / Field Supervisor

Coordinating field crews, schedules, and day-to-day farm tasks on site.

Don't see your trade listed? Apply anyway — we'll match your skills and experience to current openings.
